Augmentation Stat Glossary

Each avatar's augmentation profile includes performance metrics commonly used in cybernetic enhancement ratings:

  • Strength - Measures enhanced muscular strength, lifting and carrying capacity.
  • Neural Synch - Indicates how efficiently the brain interfaces with the cybernetic implants.
  • Optic Upgrade - Represents visual enhancement tier, such as thermal, zoom, and spectral.
  • Stamina - Tracks sustained energy output and endurance.
  • Thermal Vision - Heat-based detection capabilities.
  • Reaction Speed - Measures neural processing and reflex latency.
  • Aural Sensors - Represents hearing enhancement tier.
  • Durability - Indicates resilience against physical or environmental damage.
