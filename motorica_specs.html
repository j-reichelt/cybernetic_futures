SYSTEM ARCHIVE: MOTORICA OMNI

> ACCESS LEVEL: GRANTED
> FILE ID: M-2026-OMNI

The Motorica Omni represents a paradigm shift in cybernetic prosthetics. Unlike legacy systems that rely on electrical muscle signals (EMG), the Omni utilizes high-speed optical sensors to detect minute changes in muscle volume and blood flow. This allows for individual finger control without invasive surgery.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Sensor Architecture Optical Myography (OMG) Array (x16 Sensors)
Response Latency < 5ms (Near-Instantaneous)
Grip Force 120 PSI (Crush Mode) / 0.5 PSI (Delicate Mode)
Battery Life 72 Hours (Nuclear-Diamond Cell)
Haptic Feedback Bidirectional Neural Loop
Weight 450g (Carbon-Titanium Weave)
